# ImageGen production prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, `transparent_background: true`. No external API key or fallback CLI was used. The generated images were subsequently processed in Aseprite; these prompts do not describe mouse painting.

## Character base

References, in order: `standing.png`; user-supplied orange witch pixel-art style example.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one polished production-quality pixel-art game character, a single full-body sword-ready idle pose on true transparent background. Image 1 is the CHARACTER IDENTITY reference: ash-blonde chin-length bob, thick bangs completely covering BOTH eyes, slim dark brown headband, dark chocolate school blazer with narrow gold piping and gold buttons, white pointed collar, ochre-gold ribbon bow and pleated dark brown skirt with gold hem; brown knee boots. Image 2 is the PIXEL ART CRAFTSMANSHIP and PROPORTION reference, not the character design: match its refined expressive handmade game sprite quality, elegant silhouette, anatomical joint placement, deliberate crunchy small pixel clusters, selective dark colored outlines, warm hue-shifted shadows, 3-4 well chosen tones per material, confident pixel lighting and cloth folds. Do NOT add witch hat/cape/purple hair. Create the blonde swordswoman in a natural right-facing THREE-QUARTER battle stance, balanced feet and bent knees, two hands holding a slender straight silver sword with a gold crossguard angled gently up toward the right. Face and torso clearly face RIGHT together; a small nose/chin points right, bangs hide eyes, do not mirror head against body. Full character including boots and entire blade visible. Proportions about 3.5 heads tall, head around 28% of height, not an enormous oval head or stick limbs. Rich yet economical hair clumps, a rounded bob with a flatter fringe silhouette, subtle asymmetric locks. Crisp hand-placed pixel art like the supplied orange witch sprites, not smooth anime art, not 3D, not vector polygons. Render as a clean nearest-neighbor enlargement of a roughly 100-pixel-tall sprite, consistent square pixel grid across head, clothing, hands, and blade; no smooth gradients, no anti-aliased blur, no micro-noise. One character only, no panels, text, ground plane, palette swatches, shadow, UI or watermark. Spend the quality budget on an appealing polished character design and well-articulated pose. Image is a design-quality checkpoint for subsequent four action animations.

## Slash

References: generated `character_base.png`; supplied pixel style example.

Use case: stylized-concept. Make ONE animation sprite sheet with EXACTLY SIX full-body poses of the SAME blonde sword girl, in a precisely regular THREE-COLUMN TWO-ROW grid. Transparent background, no text/gridlines. Prefer canvas 1536x1024, six equal 512x512 cells. Image 1 is the locked character design. Image 2 is supporting pixel craftsmanship reference only. Preserve image 1's character identity, hair length, bob outline, thick eye-covering bangs, dark headband, brown/gold school uniform, bow, skirt, boots, single silver sword with gold guard, polished clustered pixel shading, proportions and detail. Do not copy the orange witch character. Every pose faces RIGHT in the same three-quarter view; face/nose must never face left and face must not suddenly become front-facing. Same scale and foot ground-line in every cell. Fit entire character AND entire blade comfortably inside each cell, with at least 30 pixels between tip and cell boundaries. No duplicated bodies, missing legs, stray weapons or cropped heads. Exactly six sequential keyframes, read left to right then second row. This sheet is one strong TWO-HANDED DIAGONAL SLASH with a returning cycle: (1) ready guard, sword angled forward-up, knees flexed; (2) anticipation, both hands raise the sword above and behind right shoulder, weight shifts to rear leg, head still clearly faces right; (3) release passing forward above shoulder height, torso drives forward and both arms accelerate the blade down-right; (4) contact/follow-through, both hands extended down-forward at waist level, sword points down-right at 25 degrees, front knee bent; (5) recovery, hands draw blade upward toward chest; (6) return to the same ready pose as frame 1. Give frames 2-5 believable distinct full-body poses, hip/shoulder rotation, knees loading and extending, and subtle bob/pleated skirt follow-through. Head design stays consistent. No sword arcs, motion trails, sparks, glow or speedlines; effects will be added in a separate Aseprite layer. Crisp refined square pixel clusters, no anti-aliased illustration, no vector cutout look, no smooth shading, no ultra-large baby head. Treat the six cells as six professional hand-animated game sprite cels.

## Blunt

References: generated `character_base.png`; supplied pixel style example.

One professional PIXEL ART animation spritesheet, EXACTLY SIX full body frames in a regular THREE column TWO row grid; landscape 1536x1024 preferred, equal slots, genuinely transparent background. Image1 locks character identity and rendering quality. Image2 is only handcrafted pixel technique reference. Blonde bob, thick eye-covering fringe, dark headband, chocolate brown school blazer gold trim, ochre bow, white collar/cuffs, gold-trimmed pleated brown skirt, brown boots, ONE silver sword with gold crossguard. All faces and bodies point screen RIGHT in the same three-quarter view; do not turn head left, do not show eyes, do not mirror character. Same scale and baseline, each whole character and full sword within its cell, ample gutters. This is a close-range blunt HILT / POMMEL STRIKE, not a sword slash or thrust. Sequence read left-to-right then second row: 1 ready two-handed guard sword angled up-right; 2 coil shoulders and bring sword upright close to the chest, elbows bent; 3 cock the hilt beside the chest with the blade pointing up-left and gold pommel leading to right; 4 a forceful short forward shoulder-and-hilt bash, both hands drive the handle / golden rounded pommel toward an opponent on the RIGHT at upper torso height while the silver BLADE points UP-LEFT away from target, front knee loads, torso leans forward, head still faces right; 5 withdraw the hilt to chest with blade upright; 6 return to the same ready stance as frame1. The forward strike is made with gold pommel, NOT a bare fist, not the sword tip. Distinct readable body poses with bent knees, natural wrists/two hands gripping one hilt, head and hands attached correctly. Keep the character stylish and expressive, 3.5 heads tall, generous hair/cloth detail with economical warm-shaded square pixel clusters and dark selective outlines. NO simplified geometric doll, smooth vectors, 3D, painting, blur, neon, effects, sparks, motion trails, text, labels, gridlines, ground, shadows or extra characters. Six isolated detailed cels only.
